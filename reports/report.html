Product by product

One panel per product, each on its own scale — a product doing 3,000 conversions and one doing 100 can’t share an axis honestly. The bars show the conversion trend; the figures are the selected month.
Cost per conversion is now attributed. Each product’s cost comes from the campaigns that actually targeted it, not from dividing the whole channel’s spend by that product’s conversions. The old method gave every product the same numerator, so it inflated every figure and made products look comparable when they weren’t. Attributed costs are therefore much lower, and they mean something. Each panel names the channels its cost covers —

Where the money actually goes

Not every dollar belongs to a product. Upper-funnel brand activity works across all of them, and some campaigns target products this report doesn’t track. Both are held separately rather than folded into a product’s cost, which would flatter it.
